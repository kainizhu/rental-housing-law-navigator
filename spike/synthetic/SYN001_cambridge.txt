CITY OF CAMBRIDGE
ORDINANCE NO. 2026-91 (FICTIONAL)
AN ORDINANCE AMENDING TITLE 8 OF THE CAMBRIDGE MUNICIPAL CODE TO ESTABLISH
TENANT STABILITY PROTECTIONS

Section 1. Chapter 8.80, Tenant Stability, is hereby added to the Cambridge Municipal Code as follows:

8.80.010 Applicability.
This Chapter applies to residential rental units located in buildings containing six or more dwelling units. This Chapter shall not apply to owner-occupied buildings.

8.80.020 Just cause required.
No landlord of a covered unit shall terminate a tenancy or seek to recover possession of a covered unit except for one of the following causes: (a) nonpayment of rent; (b) material breach of the lease; (c) nuisance; (d) the owner's good-faith intent to occupy the unit as a primary residence.

8.80.030 Annual rent increase limit.
No landlord of a covered unit shall increase the rent for the unit by more than five percent (5%) in any twelve-month period.

8.80.040 Penalties.
Any violation of this Chapter shall be subject to a fine of up to $300 per violation per day.

Section 2. Effective date.
This ordinance shall take effect on the first day of the sixth month following its adoption.

Passed to be ordained by the City Council of the City of Cambridge on September 14, 2026.
